2015 → 2026 • OUR LITTLE FOREVER

Our Story ❤️

Some people enter your life quietly… and somehow become your whole world. This is not just a collection of memories. This is our journey — from a library, a first look and a late-night SMS to dreams, distance, difficult nights and a love that still chooses each other.

2015

The First Look — Ek Nazar, Aur Dil Haar Gaya

Hum dono board exams ki preparation ke liye library aate the. Tum 10th ke boards ke liye aur main 12th ke boards ke liye. Ek din meri nazar tumhari aankhon par padi… aur pata nahi kyun, dil wahi ruk sa gaya. Us din ke baad library sirf padhne ki jagah nahi rahi. Main tumhe dekhne ke liye bhi roz library aane laga.

“That one look… changed everything.” ❤️

Waiting Until Midnight — Your Message Was My Favourite Notification

Us waqt tumhare paas apna mobile bhi nahi tha. Kabhi didi ke number se, kabhi mummy ke number se normal SMS par baat hoti thi. Main raat ke 12 baje tak tumhare message ka wait karta tha. Aur jab tumhara message aata tha, lagta tha jaise poori raat meri ho gayi.

“Those late-night conversations were the beginning of our forever.” 💗
21 February 2016

The YES — The Day I Found My Forever

2016 mein Facebook par baat aur zyada hone lagi. Phir aaya woh din jo meri life ke sabse special days mein se ek hai. 21 February 2016 ko maine tumse apne dil ki baat keh di… aur tumne YES bol diya. Us ek “YES” ne meri duniya ko aur khoobsurat bana diya.

“That day… I didn't just get a YES. I found someone I wanted beside me.” ❤️
2016 – 2018

Different Colleges, Same Love

Humne different colleges mein admission liya, lekin distance humare beech kabhi dilon ka distance nahi bana. Hum milte rahe, baatein karte rahe aur chhoti-chhoti moments ko memories banate rahe. Journey alag thi, lekin dil ek hi direction mein tha.

“Different paths… same destination — together.”
2018 – 2019

Her CA Dream — Your Dream Became My Pride

Phir tumne CA banne ka decision liya. Tumhari padhai tumhari priority ban gayi. Library mein pehle jaisi baatein nahi hoti thi — 7–8 PM tak mostly sirf study. Maine bhi seekha ki pyaar sirf saath waqt bitana nahi hota. Kabhi-kabhi pyaar ka matlab hota hai saamne wale ke dream ko samajhna, uske liye wait karna aur uski success ke liye dil se proud hona.

“Your dream became my responsibility, my prayer and my pride.” 📚❤️
2019

CA Foundation — I Cheered, You Worked Hard

Tumne CA Institute ka form bhara aur Foundation exam diya. Tum apne dream ki taraf ek-ek step leti gayi. Main har step mein tumhare saath tha — kabhi saamne, kabhi door se, lekin hamesha tumhare liye cheer karta aur dua karta raha.

“You studied hard… I prayed harder.” ❤️
Articleship — 3 Years

The Little Things I Still Miss — Tiffin & Your Maggi ❤️

IPCC ke baad tumhari 3 years ki articleship shuru hui. Career aur responsibilities badh gayi, time kam hota gaya… lekin tum meri wahi library wali Ashu rahi. Aur tumhara mere liye tiffin lana… aur tumhare haath ki Maggi — WOW! 😄❤️ Aaj bhi miss karta hoon. Kyunki woh sirf Maggi nahi thi. Usmein tumhara care, tumhara effort aur tumhara pyaar tha.

“Your Maggi was never just food… it was love served in a little bowl.” 🍜❤️
2020

Lockdown — Distance Couldn't Break Us

Corona aaya aur poori duniya ruk gayi. Hum mil nahi paate the, lekin humari baatein nahi ruki. Mobile screen hi humari mulaqat ban gayi. Us waqt samajh aaya ki saath rehna sirf paas rehna nahi hota.

“The world stopped… but my heart still found you.” ❤️
2021 – 2022

After Corona — Every Meeting Became a Memory

Sab normal hone laga toh hum phir milne lage. Gardens mein, ghar se door, thoda sa waqt chura kar. Problems thi, distance tha, restrictions thi… lekin tumse milne ka har moment worth it lagta tha.

“The meetings were short, but the memories stayed long.” 🌳❤️
A Night We Almost Lost Us

The Night I Couldn't Sleep — Because I Couldn't Lose You

Diwali ke time tumhare bhai ne humein saath dekh liya. Us raat tumhara message aaya ki shayad ab hum saath nahi reh sakte aur breakup karna hai. Woh raat main soya nahi. Main tumhe samjhata raha, explain karta raha, bas isliye kyunki dil mein ek hi baat thi — main tumhe khona nahi chahta tha. Humne mushkil waqt dekha, roye, dare… lekin phir ek baar humne ek-dusre ko choose kiya.

“We didn't have a perfect story. We had a real one — and we kept choosing each other.” 💔➡️❤️
Two Faiths, One Love

Different Faiths — Same Respect, Same Love

Main Muslim hoon, tum Hindu ho. Kabhi-kabhi future ko lekar confusion hoti hai, questions aate hain aur darr bhi lagta hai. Lekin humne ek-dusre ke faith ko change karne ki jagah ek-dusre ko samajhna seekha. Hum ek-dusre ke birthdays par Mandir aur Dargah jaate hain — do different places, lekin dil se ek hi dua: khushi, peace aur saath.

“Different faiths… same prayers… same love.” 🕌🛕❤️
2026

CA Final — I Will Wait For Your Dream

Aaj tum CA Final ki preparation mein busy ho. Pehle jaisa time nahi mil pata. Kabhi family ke saath trip par hoti ho, kabhi studies mein busy hoti ho aur baat nahi ho pati. Haan, main tumhe miss karta hoon. Bahut karta hoon. Lekin main tumhare busy time mein bhi tumse utna hi pyaar karta hoon jitna tumhare free time mein. Tum apna dream complete karo. Main wait karunga.

“Go get your dream, Ashu. I’ll always be here — cheering for you.” 📚❤️
Every Morning • Every Day

You Are My First Thought

Subah aankh khulti hai toh sabse pehle tum yaad aati ho. Office jaata hoon toh tum yaad aati ho. Din mein kuch achha hota hai toh tumse share karne ka mann karta hai. Aur jab tum family ke saath trip par hoti ho aur baat nahi karti… tab aur zyada samajh aata hai ki tum meri life ka kitna bada hissa ho.

“You are my first thought, my every thought, and somehow… my last thought too.” ☀️❤️

Zindagi chahe kitni bhi badal jaaye, behenon ke beech ka pyaar hamesha dil ke kareeb rehta hai. ❤️✨

A Mother’s Love, A Daughter’s World

Ek aisa rishta jo pyaar se shuru hota hai aur har pal ke saath aur bhi gehra hota jaata hai. ❤️ Maa apni beti ka pehla ghar hoti hai, uski sabse badi taaqat aur hamesha pyaar dene wali ek khoobsurat jagah. Kuch rishte sirf khoon se nahi bante, balki anagint yaadon, bepanah care aur us pyaar se bante hain jo zindagi bhar dil mein bana rehta hai. ❤️✨

Our Little Princess ❤️

Bhanji ghar ki woh chhoti si khushi hoti hai jo apni masoomiyat, cute si smile aur pyaari si baaton se poore ghar ko khushiyon se bhar deti hai. ❤️ Uske saath bitaya har chhota sa pal ek beautiful memory ban jaata hai. Chahe woh kitni bhi badi ho jaaye, hamare liye woh hamesha hamari chhoti si princess hi rahegi. 🥰✨

A Mother’s Love, Forever Home

Mom sirf ek rishta nahi, balki woh ehsaas hai jahan har dard mein sukoon, har mushkil mein himmat aur har khushi mein sabse pehli muskaan milti hai. ❤️ Unki mamta, unki duaayein aur unka bina kahe samajh lena zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat cheezon mein se ek hai. Chahe waqt kitna bhi badal jaaye, Maa ka pyaar hamesha dil mein wahi rahega — sabse khaas, sabse kareeb. 🥹❤️

❤️ Family, Love & Togetherness

Family ke kuch pal bahut simple hote hain, lekin wahi pal hamari zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat yaadein ban jaate hain, jinhe hum hamesha apne dil mein sambhal kar rakhte hain. ❤️ Bhai-behen ka rishta chhoti-chhoti nok-jhok, bepanah hasi, ek-doosre ki care aur us pyaar se bhara hota hai jise aksar lafzon mein kehne ki zarurat hi nahi padti. ❤️

Aur jab Mosi bhi hamare saath hoti hain, toh ye pal aur bhi zyada khaas ban jaate hain. ❤️ Ye wahi log hain jo family ko sirf ek rishta nahi, balki ek ghar jaisa ehsaas banate hain — jahan hum saath muskurate hain, ek-doosre ka khayal rakhte hain aur aisi khoobsurat yaadein banate hain jinhe hum hamesha pyaar se yaad karenge. ❤️✨

Forever ❤️ Together

Family sirf ek picture mein saath hone ka naam nahi hai, balki yeh us pyaar, hasi, chhoti-chhoti khushiyon aur un khoobsurat yaadon ka naam hai jo hum saath milkar banate hain. ❤️ Yeh wahi pal hain jinhe hum aane wale waqt mein muskurate hue yaad karenge — kyunki zindagi humein chahe kahin bhi le jaaye, family ka pyaar hamesha humein apne ghar aur apno ke paas wapas le aayega. ❤️

Hamare is khoobsurat family, anagint yaadon aur un sabhi apno ke naam, jo hamari zindagi ko sach mein special banate hain. ❤️ Bas yahi dua hai ki hamara saath hamesha bana rahe, hum hamesha saath muskurate rahein aur milkar aisi hi bahut saari khoobsurat yaadein banate rahein, jinhe hum zindagi bhar apne dil mein sambhal kar rakh sakein. ❤️✨
